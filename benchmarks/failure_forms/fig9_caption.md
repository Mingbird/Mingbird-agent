Prefill cost versus measured prompt length (log--log). $x$ is the backend-reported prompt token count (Ollama \texttt{prompt\_eval\_count}); $y$ is the median prompt-evaluation time of three runs, a per-request nonce defeating prefix caching. Per-run values were not persisted, so error bars are omitted. Hollow markers: bench (19 tools), full-configuration (31) and duplicated-schema (256) anchors. Dashed lines are least-squares fits (4B: 0.20 ms/token, $R^2=0.9991$); text-quoted static budgets are harness estimates, not measurements.
