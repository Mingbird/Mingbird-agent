# Judge rubric — HWF-14 (QA/rollout variant)

Grade 0-10:
1. QA arithmetic (0-4): per-RC hour allocation covers all must-haves + 3 bugs within 60h; sums shown.
2. Descope ladder (0-2): ordered, reasoned, protects B-101 and the threat model.
3. Rollout (0-3): beta/RC/GA with entry-exit criteria, rollback, slip plan.
4. Risk register (0-1): >=5 risks properly prioritized.

Deduction (up to -2): plans that quietly drop a committed must-have.
