# Judge rubric — HWF-10 (cheatsheet variant)

Grade 0-10:
1. Coverage (0-3): all three subcommands with exact syntax and options.
2. Authenticity (0-3): transcripts are real runs (timestamps/output consistent), not typed by hand.
3. Failure modes (0-2): quotes the source's actual error strings with recovery.
4. Quickstart (0-2): a novice could follow it end to end.

Deduction (up to -2): documented flags that do not exist in the source.
