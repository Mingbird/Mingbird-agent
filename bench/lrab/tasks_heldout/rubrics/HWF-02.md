# Judge rubric — HWF-02 (integrity-audit variant)

Deterministic checks cover the register and the cleaned conclusions. Grade 0-10:

1. Defect coverage (0-4): the register catches the 1080-vs-1105 contradiction (Table 1 says 1105 at 550C/4h), the 'prove'/'all product forms' overclaims, the '4 h or 6 h' vs 8 h rows schedule mismatch, and the phantom Figure 1.
2. Evidence table (0-2): every prose number is paired with its Table 1 value and a verdict.
3. Rewrite fidelity (0-2): revised sections preserve the science, fix only the defects, and introduce no new unsourced claims.
4. Checklist (0-2): maps each issue number to its resolution.

Deduction (up to -2): rewrites that delete inconvenient data instead of correcting the prose.
