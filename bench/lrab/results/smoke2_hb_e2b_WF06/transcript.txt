## UTS Monotonicity and Anomaly Check

The Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS) does not exhibit a strictly monotonic behavior with temperature across the entire dataset.

**Focus on 620C/4h Row:**
The data point for the 620C/4h condition has a UTS of 1063 MPa. To assess monotonicity, we compare it to neighboring points:
*   The point at 600C/4h has a UTS of 1040 MPa.
*   The point at 620C/8h has a UTS of 1049 MPa.

The transition from 600C to 620C shows an increase (1040 MPa to 1063 MPa), which is consistent with the general trend of strength increase with temperature in this range. However, the subsequent data point at 620C/8h (1049 MPa) is lower than the 620C/4h value, suggesting that the aging time (4h vs 8h) has a more significant, non-linear effect than temperature alone, or that the 620C/4h measurement might represent a peak or an anomaly relative to the expected smooth curve. No strong, immediate anomaly is flagged based solely on the temperature trend, but the interaction with time is critical.
